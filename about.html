About FMPH

Built by people who've done this work themselves

Bookkeeping, call center, sales, healthcare support, and interpretation — brought together under one accountable, worldwide remote team.

Our story

One team, six service lines, zero dropped balls

FMPH Multiservices LLC started with a simple idea: small and growing businesses shouldn't have to choose between answering the phone and closing the books. We bring bookkeeping, call center, sales, healthcare support, and interpretation together under one accountable team — so nothing falls through the cracks while you focus on running the business.

Our team works remotely and worldwide, which means we build schedules around your business hours — not the other way around. Whether a client needs a bilingual receptionist, a bookkeeper who knows QuickBooks Online inside and out, or an interpreter on standby, it's the same team, the same account manager, and the same standard of care.
